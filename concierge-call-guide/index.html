Rally Concierge call guide

Everything you need to run the Crypto Readiness Session and the Financing & Purchase Session for Elevate Smart Bundle buyers: what to check before the call, what to say, what to do in GHL, and what never to do.

Never, on any call

These protect the buyer and us. They apply to both sessions, every time.

  • Never ask for passwords, private keys, seed or recovery phrases, or one-time codes. If a buyer starts to share one, stop them.
  • Never take control of the buyer’s screen, device, exchange account or wallet, and never move funds for them. They do every click.
  • Never give financial, investment, legal or tax advice, recommend an exchange or asset, or predict rewards or returns.
  • Never give crypto guidance before the Onboarding Acknowledgment is signed.
  • Never share a wallet address yourself by chat, email or voice. The buyer copies it only from their order form.
  • Never say or show internal names. You are the Rally Concierge.

During the call

  1. Welcome and set the purpose

    2 min

    Thanks for joining. Today is about getting your company’s crypto account ready so you can make your down payment when you’re ready to buy. I’ll explain how things generally work, and the decisions are always yours.

  2. Sign the Onboarding Acknowledgment

    3 min

    Send it from GHL while you’re on the call and wait until it’s signed. Give no crypto guidance until it is.

    Before we start, I’m sending a short acknowledgment. It explains what I can help with, what I’ll never ask you for, and what you’re responsible for. Take a minute to read it, and let me know when it’s signed.

    In GHL: confirm the document shows as signed on the contact. The esb-onboarding-ack-signed tag is added.

  3. Work through only what they flagged

    15–20 min

    Use their questionnaire answers. Skip anything they already have in place.

    • No account, or not in the company’s name: explain that most major exchanges offer business accounts and that the account must be in the company’s name. They choose the exchange.
    • Not fully verified: finishing business verification is usually what raises the sending limit.
    • Sending limit below the down payment: show where limits are usually found in account settings and how increases are requested. Increases can take days, so start now.
    • Needs to buy crypto: the down payment is USDT or USDC only. Newly bought crypto can be held for a few days before it can be sent, so buy early.
    • Never sent to an outside wallet: walk through how a transfer works: copy and paste the address (never type it), check the network, check the amount. Suggest practicing with a small amount between their own accounts.

    Don’t suggest a test transfer to Connect’s wallet, and don’t share any wallet address.

  4. Agree next steps

    3 min

    Recap what they’ll do and roughly when. Explain what happens next.

    You’ll get an email today with an “I’m crypto ready” button. Click it when your company’s account can send the down payment. Your answers are already filled in, so you just update what’s changed. Then you’ll book your Financing & Purchase Session and preview your quote.

After the call

  • Mark the appointment Showed in GHL. This sends the “I’m crypto ready” email, with reminders after 3 and 7 days until they come back ready.
  • If they didn’t join, mark No-show. They get a link to rebook.
  • Add a short note on the contact: what they still need to do and any dates they gave.

During the call

  1. Welcome and agenda

    2 min

    Today we’ll confirm your final quote, go through your agreement while you sign it, and then I’ll stay with you while you send your down payment. You’ll do every step yourself; I’ll guide you.

  2. Onboarding Acknowledgment, if not signed

    3 min

    Buyers who skipped the Crypto Readiness Session sign it now, before anything else. Same wording as in the readiness session.

  3. Confirm the final quote

    8 min

    Open the Finalize Quote link and enter the team passcode. It’s pre-filled from their Quote Preview and shows the live Rally SmartNode price and licenses left at that price right now.

    Your preview was based on the price at the time. Let’s confirm your final numbers: is [X] bundles over [term] months still what you’d like? The price you see now is the one your agreement will lock when you sign.

    • Check the company’s legal name, the authorized signer and title, and the address with them. The signer also signs the personal guaranty.
    • Change the quantity or term if they want to, and walk through the updated figures and payment schedule.
    • When they confirm, click Finalize quote and send agreement. If the price changed since you opened the page, you’ll see the new price; confirm it with them and finalize again.

    Don’t share the Finalize Quote link or the passcode with the buyer. Only you finalize the quote.

  4. Walk through the agreement while they sign

    10 min

    The agreement arrives in their email within a minute or two. With it open on their screen, point out: the purchase figures (Schedule A), the payment schedule (Schedule B), that every payment is made in USDT or USDC only, the personal guaranty, and that there are no guarantees about rewards or returns.

    For any question about what a term means legally, please check with your own counsel. I can walk you through the figures and the process.

    They sign. If the price changed since the quote, a new agreement is sent automatically: review the new figures together, then they sign that one.

  5. Send the down payment

    10 min

    Open the order form with them. It shows the exact amount, the token and network, and Connect’s receiving wallet.

    • They choose USDT or USDC and send on the network shown on the form.
    • They copy and paste the address from the order form (it matches the Seller Wallet Address in Schedule A of their signed agreement), then check the first and last few characters match.
    • If they’ve never sent to an outside wallet (“Sent before: No”), go slowly: confirm the network together, and check the first and last characters out loud before they press send.
    • Network fees are theirs, and only the amount received counts, so the amount that arrives must cover the full down payment.
    • Stay on the call until the form shows the payment confirmed.

    Please copy the address from your order form only. If you ever receive a different address by email or message, don’t use it. Check with us first.

  6. Wrap up

    3 min
    • Their payment receipt arrives by email.
    • Connect countersigns and emails the fully signed agreement.
    • Licenses are issued within 10 business days.
    • Their first monthly payment date is in their agreement, and they’ll get access to the financing portal.

If they can’t finish the payment on the call

  • Find out why: sending limit, a hold on newly bought crypto, or verification.
  • Book a follow-up before the payment deadline and note the reason on the contact. The esb-payment-pending tag stays until payment is verified.
  • The agreement and price stay as signed. If the deadline passes, the agreement is voided and they start again.

After the call

  • Mark the appointment Showed (or No-show, which sends a rebooking link and alerts the team).
  • Check the deal moved forward: agreement signed, payment verified, order placed.
  • Add a note on the contact with anything the team should know.

How to answer common questions

Is this an investment?
No. It’s a purchase of software licenses, financed by Connect.This is a software license purchase. I can’t give investment, financial, legal or tax advice, so for those questions please speak with your own advisors.
How much will I earn in rewards?
Never estimate or imply a number.Rewards depend on operating your nodes and on the network. The agreement makes no guarantees about rewards, returns or profit.
Can I pay by wire, card or bank transfer?
No. Under the agreement, every payment, including the down payment and monthly payments, is made in USDT or USDC.
Can you send it for me, or take over my screen?
I can’t. You do every step yourself, and I’ll guide you through it.
Can I buy as an individual?
Purchases are made by a company or other business entity. The person signing for the company also signs a personal guaranty.
When is my price locked?
The moment you sign your agreement. Until then the quote uses the current live price.
What about taxes?
I can’t advise on taxes. The agreement recommends speaking with your own CPA, especially about paying in crypto and about rewards.
Someone sent me a new wallet address. Should I use it?
Treat it as a warning sign.Don’t use it. Only use the address on your order form. Any change to Connect’s wallet comes with written notice at least 10 business days ahead, and you’d confirm it with us another way first.

When something goes wrong

Less than the full down payment arrivedNote the amount and transaction hash and post in the team channel. Don’t tell them how to fix it until the team confirms the rule.
They want to change bundles or the term after signingPost in the team channel before doing anything. A signed agreement can’t be edited on the call.
