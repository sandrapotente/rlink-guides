How it works in a minute

When a doctor finishes a visit and wants to bring in someone for a follow-up, they click Request Specialist. Your call center gets an email with a Claim this call button. The first person to claim it joins the doctor’s Clinic Room through the waiting room, the doctor lets them in, and the specialist takes over the call.

The patient never changes rooms, clicks a new link, or waits in a queue. They stay in the same call from the first hello to the last.

Who’s involved

Four people play a part in every handoff.

You, the agency

Set the email address where handoff alerts go, and decide whether doctors can change it.

The doctor

Runs the visit, requests a specialist when needed, and lets the specialist into the room.

The patient

Joins the doctor’s Clinic Room once and stays there for the whole call.

The specialist

A call center rep or the doctor’s assistant who claims the handoff and takes over the conversation.

Setting up

Do this once, before your doctors start using handoffs.

In your agency account

Agency Account › Settings › Warm Handoff Email

  • Default Handoff Email: enter the inbox your call center watches. Every doctor account you create uses this address automatically.
  • Allow doctors to change handoff email: leave this off to send every handoff to your call center. Turn it on to let doctors use their own address instead, for example an assistant’s.
  • Click Save Handoff Email.

If you change the default later, every doctor still using it updates automatically. Doctors who set their own email keep theirs. Make sure the default is always filled in, or handoff requests won’t reach anyone.

In each doctor’s account

  • Rooms are called Clinic Rooms. They have all the usual R-Link room settings, plus Warm Handoff.
  • The doctor’s settings show the handoff email. If you’ve allowed changes, the doctor can set one custom email, or use Reset to default to go back to yours.
  • Each doctor’s alerts go to exactly one email address.

A handoff, step by step

The green line is the patient. Notice it never breaks.

Patient stays in the Clinic Room
1
Doctor

The visit happens as usual

The doctor and patient meet in one of the doctor’s Clinic Rooms. If no follow-up is needed, the doctor ends the call like any other.

2
Doctor

The doctor clicks Request Specialist

The button changes to Handoff requested and can’t be clicked again, so your team never gets a duplicate alert. The doctor keeps talking with the patient.

The patient sees: “A specialist will be joining shortly.”
3
Specialist

Your team gets the email

The email shows the doctor’s name, the Clinic Room, and the time, with a Claim this call button. It contains no patient details, so it’s safe for a shared inbox.

4
Specialist

The first person to claim it takes the call

They click Claim this call, type their name, and land in the doctor’s waiting room. No login is needed. Anyone else who opens the link sees “This handoff has already been claimed.”

5
Doctor

The doctor lets the specialist in

The specialist appears in the waiting room with the name they typed. The doctor admits them whenever they’re ready. If the doctor needs a few more minutes with the patient, the specialist simply waits.

The moment they’re admitted, the specialist automatically becomes host of the room.

6
Doctor

A warm introduction, then the doctor leaves

All three can talk together while the doctor introduces the specialist. When the doctor is ready, they leave. The call carries on for the patient and specialist.

While the specialist is in there, that Clinic Room shows as In use on the doctor’s dashboard. The doctor can go straight into another Clinic Room for their next patient.

7
Specialist

The specialist ends the call

When the conversation is done, the specialist ends the call. Their host access ends with it, and the Clinic Room is free for the doctor again.

What the specialist can do

As temporary host, the specialist gets what they need to run the conversation, and nothing that would expose the patient’s earlier visit.

ActionAllowed
End the callYes
Mute or unmute participantsYes
Remove a participantYes
Share their screenYes
Record the callNo
See chat or files from before they joinedNo
Change room settingsNo
Let new people in from the waiting roomNo

Host access is for that one call only. The specialist never gets access to the doctor’s room, account, or settings afterward.

When things don’t go to plan

Every situation below is handled automatically. Here’s what happens.

The specialist loses their connection

They have 60 seconds to reconnect, and they come back as host. While they’re gone, host passes back to the doctor if the doctor is still in the room. If the doctor already left, the patient sees “Reconnecting you with a specialist.”

After 60 seconds, the same claim link opens up again, so someone else on your team can claim it from the original email.

Nobody claims the handoff

If no one claims it within 10 minutes, the request expires and the link stops working. The doctor sees “No one claimed this handoff” and can send a new request or simply continue the call.

Two people click Claim at the same time

Only one gets in. The other sees “This handoff has already been claimed,” so there’s never any doubt about who’s taking the call.

The doctor changes their mind

The doctor can cancel a pending request without ending the call. The link in the email stops working right away, and anyone who opens it sees “This session has ended.”

The doctor needs to leave before anyone joins

A room always needs a host, so until a specialist has been admitted, the doctor’s only way out is to end the call for everyone. That quietly cancels the handoff. A patient is never left alone in a room with no host.

The next patient arrives at a room that’s in use

They wait in that room’s waiting room. They can’t walk in on someone else’s conversation, because the specialist can’t admit new people. They’ll be let in once that call ends and the doctor opens the room. To avoid the wait, see the first tip for doctors below.

Tips for your team

For doctors

  • Give back-to-back patients different Clinic Room links, so the next patient never lands in a room where a handoff is still running.
  • Use the few moments after admitting the specialist for a quick introduction. It makes the handover feel seamless to the patient.
  • If you click Request Specialist by mistake, just cancel it.

For your call center

  • Keep the handoff inbox open with notifications on. Patients are live on the call while they wait.
  • Type your real name when you claim. It’s what the doctor and patient see.
  • If your connection drops, rejoin from the same email within 60 seconds to keep the call.
